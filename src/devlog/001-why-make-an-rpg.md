# Why make an RPG when you make daily games?

Status: admitted out loud. Devlog started.

We started Daymode with a simple promise: two minutes, one puzzle, every day. Five Degrees shipped first — connect two movies through the actors they share, a fresh pair every midnight UTC, ten hops max, results you can share like Wordle. Mini Murder is next in the case file: one small crime, solved over coffee, daily.

So why an RPG? Because the daily games are the gym. This is the marathon.

- Scope exists to be feared. We fear it. We are doing it anyway.
- The daily games are a two-minute focus group. What players enjoy in two minutes becomes the RPG's design doc.
- No name yet. That's your first hint it's early days.

This devlog is where the big one gets built in public: what we're making, why, and how it's going. Warts, wins, and rewrites included.

First real build notes go up once there is something to show.
